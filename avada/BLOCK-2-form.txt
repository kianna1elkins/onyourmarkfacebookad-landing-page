================================================================
AVADA ROUTE  ·  BLOCK 2 of 3  ·  your Avada form
================================================================

This block is NOT a Custom HTML block. A shortcode will NOT run
inside a Custom HTML block, so the form needs its own block type.

STEP 1 — Get your form's shortcode
----------------------------------
1. In WordPress admin, go to:  Avada  ->  Forms
2. Click your form to open it.
3. Look at your browser's address bar. The URL contains something
   like  ...post.php?post=1234&action=edit
   That number (1234) is your FORM ID.
4. Your shortcode is:

       [fusion_form form_post_id="1234" /]

   (Replace 1234 with your real form ID. If Avada shows a ready-made
   shortcode anywhere, use that instead.)

STEP 2 — Add it to the page BETWEEN block 1 and block 3
-------------------------------------------------------
1. On the page, click the + to add a new block.
2. Search for "Shortcode" and choose the Shortcode block.
3. Paste your  [fusion_form form_post_id="1234" /]  into it.
4. Make sure the block order top-to-bottom is:

       Custom HTML   = BLOCK-1-top.html
       Shortcode     = your Avada form  (this step)
       Custom HTML   = BLOCK-3-bottom.html

STEP 3 — Make the form email Mark
---------------------------------
Open the form in Avada -> Forms -> (your form) -> Form Options ->
"Confirmation / Email" (wording varies by Avada version) and set the
notification "Send To" email to:

       mark@onyourmarktransportation.com

Avada also stores every submission under Avada -> Form Entries, so
you have a backup even if an email is missed.

TIP — width / centering
-----------------------
If the form looks too wide, open the form's settings in Avada and set
a max width (around 640px) and center alignment so it matches the
heading above it.
